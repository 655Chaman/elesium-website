## 1. The Core Law of Compact Keywords

> **The Fundamental Theorem:**  
> *"If you rank #1 for one compact keyword, you will rank #1–3 for dozens of related compact keywords across your semantic cluster."*

Legacy SEO agencies focus on high-volume, Top-of-Funnel (TOFU) keywords (e.g., *"What is Artificial Intelligence"*, *"Best CRM software"*). These queries:
- Are monopolized by high-Domain-Authority (DA 90+) aggregators (Forbes, G2, HubSpot).
- Have essentially **0% commercial purchase intent** (users are researching homework or definitions).
- Cost thousands of dollars in backlinks and take 12–18 months to rank.

In contrast, **Compact Keywords** target the absolute bottom of the funnel (BOFU) where enterprise buyers and decision-makers are ready to buy:
- **Search Volume:** 10 to 50 searches per month (completely ignored by competitors).
- **Purchase Intent:** 90%+ commercial conversion rate.
- **Authority Barrier:** Sites with DA < 10 routinely capture positions 1–3 within days.
- **Cost-to-Rank:** Near zero when structured according to Edward Sturm's mathematical on-page geometry.

## 2. The 5 Properties of a Compact Keyword

Every page generated by this factory must satisfy all five properties:

1. **Compact Competition:**  
   Very few competitors target the query because monthly search volume looks superficially unattractive to traditional SEO tools.
2. **Compact Authority:**  
   Search engine algorithms reward exact semantic matching and satisfaction over raw backlink authority. Low-DA sites win effortlessly.
3. **Compact Words:**  
   **Strictly 350 to 500 words.** Not 3,000 words. Not 1,500 words. Long-form content signals informational (TOFU) intent and triggers Google's automated demotion algorithms for commercial queries. High-density, compact pages signal decision-stage intent.
4. **Compact Content:**  
   Templated, modular on-page components reused programmatically across hundreds of URLs. The visual framework remains identical; only the data points, entities, and capability metrics change.
5. **Compact Cost:**  
   Zero educational overhead. The buyer already knows their problem and is comparing concrete implementation architectures and pricing.

## 5. The 6 Edward Sturm Compliance Gates

Every page generated by `generator_engine.py` is audited by `compliance_gatekeeper.py` against these six non-negotiable gates:

### Gate 1: Tri-Alignment (100% Strict Match)
The target keyword string must match across three core elements:
1. URL slug: `/solutions/[exact-keyword-slug]`
2. Meta `<title>` tag: `[Exact Keyword] | [Brand Name]`
3. Single `<h1>` tag: `[Exact Keyword] — [Operational Capability Subtitle]`

### Gate 2: Word Count & Micro-Paragraph Discipline
- Total page body word count must strictly fall between **350 and 500 words** (tolerance: 320 to 520 max).
- Every paragraph must consist of **1 to 3 sentences** (predominantly single-sentence punchy lines).
- Never allow large walls of text; mobile bounce rates skyrocket if a paragraph exceeds 4 lines on a smartphone screen.

### Gate 3: The Moz Sweet Spot (Target 96–98 / 100)
- The exact keyword must appear in:
  - The first sentence of the intro paragraph.
  - The meta description.
  - The first image `alt` attribute.
  - One `<h2>` subheading.
- **The Deliberate Incompletion Law:** Intentionally avoid a score of 100/100 on automated SEO checkers. A score of 100 indicates mechanical over-optimization and triggers search engine spam filters (`QualityTwiddler`). Aim for 96–98.

### Gate 4: Mobile Viewport Geometry
- On mobile devices, the visual hierarchy in the DOM must be:
  `H1 -> Intro Answer Capsule -> Primary CTA Button -> Graphic/Diagram`.
- The CTA button must **never** be pushed below the fold by a hero illustration or stock graphic.

### Gate 5: Crawl Depth & Orphan Prevention
- Every compact keyword landing page must exist within **$\le 2$ clicks** of the homepage.
- Achieved by mounting a centralized `/solutions` or `/capabilities` index hub and linking top clusters directly in the footer and main navigation.

### Gate 6: Semantic Static Pre-Rendering (SSG) & Canonical Binding
- Every page must be delivered to search engine crawlers as pre-rendered semantic HTML (not blank JavaScript SPAs).
- Every page must contain a self-referential `<link rel="canonical" href="https://domain.com/solutions/slug">`.

## 6. The Platform Extension Law

> *"A compact keyword must never live on your website alone. It must exist across a minimum of two digital surfaces."*

For every target compact keyword:
1. **Surface 1:** The canonical `.com` or `.online` landing page.
2. **Surface 2:** A secondary entity citation on an authoritative platform:
   - A Reddit post or comment answering a user in that niche.
   - A LinkedIn article or post targeting the exact keyword string.
   - A technical teardown on HackerNoon, Dev.to, or GitHub.
   - A YouTube video title containing the keyword.

This dual-surface footprint signals to Google's knowledge graph that the keyword represents a recognized entity concept, accelerating indexing from weeks to under 48 hours.
