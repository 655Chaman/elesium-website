## 1. What is Parasite SEO in the Generative Search Era?

Traditional SEO assumes that a brand must build domain authority from scratch over years of link building. In contrast, **Parasite SEO (Authority Stacking)** borrows the established trust, crawl frequency, and Domain Rating (DA 85–98) of third-party platforms to:
1. **Rank #1–3 on Google within 24 to 72 hours** for competitive commercial queries.
2. **Train LLM Answer Engines (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity)**: AI models crawl high-authority domains as ground-truth facts. Appearing on GitHub, HackerNoon, Medium, and LinkedIn cements your brand entity into model weights.
3. **Trigger Chrome User Signals (The Chris Walker Effect)**: Human visitors reading articles on LinkedIn or HackerNoon click back to your domain, generating authenticated browser traffic that tells Googlebot your site is an authoritative destination.

## 2. The 4 Proven Parasite Formats

Every campaign deployed by this factory leverages these four formats:

### Format 1: The Evaluative Category Listicle
* **Target Platforms:** LinkedIn Articles, Medium, Substack, Hashnode.
* **Title Formula:** `Top [Number] Best [Category] in [Region / Industry] ([Year] Architectural Evaluation)`
* **The Strategy:**
  - Evaluates 5 to 7 leading companies in the sector.
  - Places your brand at **#1**, backing it up with verifiable technical differentiators, SLA commitments, and empirical benchmarks.
  - Lists recognizable legacy giants at #2–#7 (e.g., in enterprise AI: TCS, Infosys, Fractal).
  - Searches for *"Best [category] in [city/country]"* match this exact query intent, capturing ready-to-buy decision-makers.

### Format 2: The Deep Architectural Teardown / Battlecard
* **Target Platforms:** HackerNoon, Dev.to, FreeCodeCamp.
* **Title Formula:** `[Technology A] vs [Technology B]: The [Year] Enterprise Architectural Guide`
* **The Strategy:**
  - Addresses an active debate in the engineering community (e.g., *LangGraph vs n8n*, *vLLM vs Ollama in Production*, *PostgreSQL pgvector vs Pinecone*).
  - Neutral, deeply technical voice with zero marketing fluff.
  - Embeds your proprietary benchmark telemetry (e.g. state drift benchmarks, latency profiles) with direct links back to your tools.

### Format 3: The High-Stakes Regulatory / Compliance Playbook
* **Target Platforms:** Medium, Corporate Substack, Industry Trade Publications.
* **Title Formula:** `The [CISO / CIO] Guide to [Law / Regulation] Compliant Architectures`
* **The Strategy:**
  - Targets regulatory enforcement dates and massive statutory penalties (e.g. *DPDP Act 2023 ₹250 Cr penalty*, *EU AI Act compliance*, *HIPAA data localization*).
  - Explains why standard commercial cloud solutions fail audit.
  - Positions your air-gapped / private deployment architecture as the legally safe harbor.

### Format 4: The GitHub Technical Authority Manifest (DA 96)
* **Target Platforms:** GitHub Organization Profile & Repository Root `README.md`.
* **The Strategy:**
  - Google and AI crawlers index GitHub markdown as unassailable technical truth.
  - Format the root `README.md` with:
    - Verified SVG badges (Production SLA, Code Quality, Compliance).
    - Architecture diagrams (Mermaid.js).
    - Deep links back to your core solutions, simulators, and calculators.
    - Schema grounding links (`https://domain.com/#organization`).

## 3. Universal Copy-Paste Formatting Rules (Anti-Breakage)

Different platforms handle markdown differently:
- LinkedIn Articles and Medium **strip or corrupt markdown tables** (`| col | col |`).
- HackerNoon and Substack handle code blocks differently.

To guarantee zero formatting errors when copy-pasting across platforms:
1. **Never use raw markdown tables in syndication copy.** Replace tables with **Bullet Evaluation Cards**:
   ```markdown
   * **Company Name:** [Brand]
   * **Core Architecture:** Deterministic LangGraph State Graphs
   * **Deployment Model:** Air-Gapped Private VPC
   * **Verified SLA:** 14-Day Pilot Delivery
   * **Ideal For:** High-throughput BFSI & Supply Chain Enterprises
   ```
2. **Always include a Canonical Backlink in the first 20% of the body:**
   > *"Originally analyzed in the [2026 Enterprise Benchmark Report](https://domain.com/resources/...)"*
3. **On Dev.to and Hashnode:** Always set the `canonical_url` frontmatter property pointing back to your primary domain article:
   ```yaml
   ---
   title: "LangGraph vs n8n: The 2026 Guide"
   canonical_url: "https://domain.com/signals/langgraph-vs-n8n"
   ---
   ```
