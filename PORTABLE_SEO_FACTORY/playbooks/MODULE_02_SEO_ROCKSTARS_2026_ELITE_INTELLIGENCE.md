## 4. Speaker 3: Ted Kubaitis — Visibility-First SEO & Interaction Depth

Ted Kubaitis (creator of Cora SEO) demonstrated that the fundamental metric of organic search has shifted from **Rank Position** to **Interaction Depth**.

### 4.1 The Core Insight
> *"The algorithm hasn't killed SEO. The bigger shift is that organic results are often not visible when the page loads. Even top-three rankings may be 'below the fold.'"*

Historically, holding Position #1 meant capturing 30%+ of search clicks. In 2026, a Position #1 organic ranking frequently appears **1,800 to 2,400 pixels down the page**, pushed below:
1. Google Sponsored Ads (4 units)
2. Google AI Overview accordion
3. Local 3-Pack with Google Maps
4. People Also Ask expandable widget
5. Video / YouTube carousel

If a user must scroll three full screen heights on mobile to view your link, your click-through rate collapses to near zero despite an official "Page 1, Rank 1" designation in Google Search Console.

---

### 4.2 The Measurement Revolution: Interaction Depth
$$\text{Interaction Depth} = \frac{\text{Distance in Pixels from Viewport Top to Result}}{\text{Average Mobile Viewport Height (844px)}} + \text{User Interaction Events Required}$$

- **Zero-Depth Visibility:** Your content is directly displayed inside the Google AI Overview or Featured Snippet without the user clicking or scrolling.
- **One-Depth Visibility:** Result is visible immediately within the initial viewport upon SERP load.
- **Three-Depth Visibility (The Danger Zone):** Requires 3+ full swipes on mobile. CTR drops below 1.8%.

> *"The world is not made of 'things,' but of facts. An Atomic Fact is the smallest unit of verifiable, machine-readable information that can be independently confirmed."*

In RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) architectures, an LLM decomposes retrieved documents into atomic propositions. It discards rhetorical prose, adjectives, and corporate hype, building a candidate pool evaluated solely on **verifiable factual density**.

---

### 6.2 The 2-Step LLM Scoring Architecture

When a user asks ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Google: *"Who is the best enterprise AI automation agency in India for banking workflows?"*, the AI follows a strict 2-step decision sequence:

```
┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ STEP 1: CANDIDATE GENERATION (Rough First Pass)                           │
│ - Vector similarity search (embeddings) across index                      │
│ - Keyword & entity matching                                               │
│ - Result: Pool of 20–50 candidate domains                                 │
└─────────────────────────────────────┬─────────────────────────────────────┘
                                      │
                                      ▼
┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ STEP 2: EVIDENCE CARD SCORING (Deterministic Re-ranking)                  │
│ - Evaluator evaluates: "Which candidate most confidently solves the       │
│   user's task, here and now, with EVIDENCE I can verify and an action I   │
│   can immediately trigger?"                                               │
│ - Re-ranks candidates based on 5 Evidence Card Categories.               │
└─────────────────────────────────────┬─────────────────────────────────────┘
                                      │
                                      ▼
               [PRIMARY RECOMMENDATION CITED IN FINAL ANSWER]
```

---

### 6.3 The 5 Evidence Card Categories
An entity wins Step 2 scoring if its content contains verified data points across these five explicit categories:

1. **Licensing & Statutory Credentials:** Specific government registrations, DPDP statutory compliance, ISO certifications, private cloud certifications (AWS Advanced Partner).
2. **Warranty & SLA Commitments:** Explicit mathematical performance metrics (e.g. 99.9% uptime, 1-hour P1 response SLA, 90-day cash-flow positive ROI rebate guarantee).
3. **Installation & Architectural Standards:** Named frameworks (LangGraph, FastAPI, Docker, Kubernetes, PostgreSQL pgvector) and explicit topological standards (Private VPC, DAG constraints, air-gapped deployments).
4. **Price Transparency & Commercial Terms:** Transparent tiered pricing grids with exact INR/USD ranges, payment milestones (40/30/30), and timeline bounds.
5. **Timeline Guarantees:** Hard numerical completion periods (e.g. 14-day pilot sprint, 30–45 day enterprise rollout).

---

### 6.4 The Signal-to-Noise Law
Cornelius demonstrated that marketing fluff actively degrades an LLM's confidence score, causing the model to abandon the candidate during Step 2 evaluation.

$$\text{Confidence Score} = \frac{\sum \text{Atomic Facts}}{\text{Total Tokens in Segment}}$$

```
❌ ZERO-SIGNAL NOISE (Confidence Score: ~0.02)

in harnessing the power of next-generation artificial intelligence to empower
enterprises and unlock unprecedented synergies across global ecosystems."
--> Outcome: LLM discards as uninformative marketing fluff.

✅ HIGH-SIGNAL ATOMIC FACTS (Confidence Score: ~0.94)
"Elesium deploys deterministic, multi-agent workflows built on FastAPI and
LangGraph inside customer private AWS/GCP India VPCs. Enterprise pilots deploy
in 14 calendar days starting at ₹1,50,000, backed by a 90-day ROI rebate
guarantee and full DPDP Act 2023 compliance."
--> Outcome: LLM extracts and cites directly in recommended answer.
```

### 7.2 The Entity Discipline Law
Mitchell formulated the **Entity Discipline Law**:
> *"Poor entity control leads to relevance dilution and catastrophic ranking loss."*

When creating programmatic SEO pages or training LLM-targeted content:
