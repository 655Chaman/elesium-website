Common Questions

How teams work with us

How does Elesium keep company data private?

Every system runs inside your own private cloud (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud) or on your on-premise servers. Customer records and internal documents never leave your network or go to external consumer AI services.

How quickly can an initial workflow go live?

A focused pilot for a single workflow is built and tested in your staging environment within 14 days, so your team can measure speed and accuracy before expanding.

What systems can you connect with?

We integrate directly with SAP, Oracle, core banking systems, PostgreSQL, internal APIs, spreadsheets, and document pipelines using structured validation checks before any record is updated.

How does the 90-day guarantee work?

Before building, we agree on clear baseline numbers - hours saved, error rates, or turnaround time. If the production system fails to deliver the agreed financial return within 90 days of launch, our fees are rebated.
