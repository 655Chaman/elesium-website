## 🎯 Executive Overview

This Google Sheets-based tracker provides a bird's-eye view of your entire lead pipeline—from the first sign of interest to a booked high-ticket sales call. It automatically aggregates data from your daily efforts across LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, and TikTok into a beautiful, hands-off dashboard.

### Why Implement This Tracker?

- **🧭 Omnichannel Clarity**: Stop losing leads in the DMs. Track prospects seamlessly across 7 different channels in one centralized location.
- **🤖 Automated Analytics**: The master Dashboard requires *zero* manual input. Just log your daily interactions in the monthly tabs, and let the sheet calculate your conversion metrics dynamically.
- **🔎 Micro-Conversion Tracking**: Pinpoint exactly where leads drop off with granular stage-to-stage conversion rates (A→B, B→C, C→D).

---

## 🏗️ The 4-Stage Pipeline

This tracker is built on a proven 4-step conversion architecture tailored for high-ticket acquisition:

1. **(A) Interested**: The prospect asked a question, made a request, or commented on your inbound content.
2. **(B) Engaged**: You've initiated a deeper conversation (e.g., successfully sent your Native VSL).
3. **(C) Calendly'd**: You've successfully dropped your booking link.
4. **(D) Booked**: The prospect secured a time on your calendar!

## 🚀 Implementation Guide

### 1. Secure Your Copy
Make a private copy of the [Multi-Channel Tracker](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1-aLyIWilXpnhshXDA2krm98a0uSCdSt_jaC0xx3unDA/edit?usp=sharing) to your own Google Drive workspace by clicking **File > Make a copy**.

### 2. Daily Operations (Monthly Tabs)
Navigate to the current month's tab (e.g., `Jan`, `Feb`). This is your daily operating system.
- When a prospect shows interest, add their name, date, and platform to the **Interested (A)** column.
- As they move through the funnel, log their details to the subsequent columns (**Engaged**, **Calendly'd**, **Booked**).
- **Workflow Pro-Tips:** Keep your sheet clean to maintain pipeline hygiene!
  - *Successfully Converted*: Strikethrough the text (do not delete the record).
  - *Not Interested / Disqualified*: Strikethrough the text and highlight the cell in **Red**.

### 3. Review the Master Dashboard
Click over to the **Dashboard** tab to view your macro-level analytics. 
- **⚠️ Do not edit this tab!** It pulls all data dynamically from your monthly sheets using advanced formulas.
- Review your **A→B**, **B→C**, and **C→D** conversion percentages to identify which platforms yield the highest quality leads, and where your appointment setters might need coaching.

---

## 📈 Advanced Metric Definitions

* **ABR %**: Overall Appointment Booking Rate.
* **A → B % (Interest to Engagement)**: Measures how effectively you transition casual interest into a real, meaningful conversation.
* **B → C % (Engagement to Link Sent)**: Measures how well your VSL or direct pitch performs at generating a calendar invite request.
* **C → D % (Link Sent to Booked)**: Measures your link-click follow-through rate. If this is low, leads are clicking your link but experiencing friction (e.g., calendar is too full, wrong time zones).
