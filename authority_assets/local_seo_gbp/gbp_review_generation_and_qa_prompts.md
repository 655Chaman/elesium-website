# Google Business Profile (GBP) Review Generation & Q&A Protocol

## 1. The Local 3-Pack Review Velocity Formula (Module 08)
Google's local map pack algorithm weighs review signals across three critical dimensions:
1. **Review Quantity & Velocity**: Consistent acquisition of verified reviews (rather than sudden bursts).
2. **Review Sentiment & Star Rating**: Sustained $\ge 4.8 / 5.0$ rating across all regional nodes.
3. **Keyword & Entity Density in Review Text**: User reviews containing explicit geo-tokens (e.g., "Bangalore", "BKC", "Hinjawadi", "HITEC City") and service phrases (e.g., "deterministic LangGraph agents", "private VPC deployment", "DPDP compliance") trigger algorithmic relevance boosts in Google's semantic map index.
