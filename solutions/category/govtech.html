Frequently Asked Questions

How does Elesium guarantee complete air-gapped security for defense deployments?
Our systems operate on bare-metal hardware with physical network diodes, disabled wireless chips, and hardware security modules (HSM) that verify cryptographic boot states. Zero internet connections exist.
Does the system qualify under the Make in India and DAP 2020 indigenization mandates?
Yes. 100% of our model architectures, state machine code, and orchestration logic are designed and maintained in India, fulfilling all IDDM (Indigenously Designed, Developed and Manufactured) criteria.
Can the agents interface with tactical military radios and MIL-STD databuses?
Yes. We support MIL-STD-1553, STANAG 4586, and tactical VHF/UHF tactical radio IP networks for low-bandwidth, high-reliability command propagation.
How are software updates and security patches delivered to air-gapped facilities?
Updates are delivered via cryptographically signed offline physical media with multi-party threshold signature verification before kernel installation.
What is the computational footprint required for tactical edge deployment?
Our tactical edge modules are optimized to run on ruggedized conduction-cooled military mission computers equipped with NVIDIA Jetson AGX Orin or compact workstation GPUs.
