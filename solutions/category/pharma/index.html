Deterministic Multi-Agent Architecture

In pharmaceutical manufacturing, a single batch record discrepancy or chromatography audit trail gap can trigger an FDA Form 483 warning letter, product recalls, or import alerts that cost hundreds of millions of dollars. Elesium deploys deterministic multi-agent auditing pipelines engineered specifically to enforce ALCOA+ (Attributable, Legible, Contemporaneous, Original, Accurate) data integrity standards across all manufacturing and laboratory systems.

Our Batch Record Review Agent ingests complex Electronic Batch Manufacturing Records (eBMR) spanning 500 to 2,000 pages of scanned weigh-dispense receipts, autoclave charts, in-process testing logs, and analytical certificates. The agent cross-checks every temperature excursion, hold-time limit, and sign-off sequence against master manufacturing formulas in sub-3 minutes, highlighting deviations for QA review.

In quality control laboratories, our Chromatography Audit Agent continuously analyzes raw audit logs from Empower, Chromeleon, and ChemStation CDS systems. It detects manual peak integration anomalies, orphaned sample injections, and unauthorized baseline manipulations, ensuring full statutory compliance with US FDA 21 CFR Part 11 electronic records mandates.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Elesium comply with US FDA 21 CFR Part 11 electronic records regulations?
Every audit event, user interaction, and automated review generates an immutable, cryptographically hashed audit trail containing timestamp, user identity, reason for change, and system fingerprint.
Can the agents interface with our Waters Empower 3 or Thermo Chromeleon CDS?
Yes. We ingest raw CDS database audit logs via non-invasive read-only ODBC connectors and automated export daemons without altering underlying chromatography files.
What happens when an automated batch audit identifies a critical quality deviation?
The system automatically pauses batch release progression in the LIMS/MES, creates a formal deviation ticket with all relevant sensor traces, and notifies QA directors immediately.
How does the system ensure zero hallucinations when compiling regulatory filings?
All extracted numerical values in regulatory dossiers must carry a deterministic pointer to their original laboratory raw data source document with strict arithmetic cross-verification.
What is the typical time savings for a commercial pharmaceutical QA department?
Commercial manufacturing plants typically reduce their batch record review cycle from 7 to 10 calendar days down to under 24 hours, releasing inventory to market significantly faster.
