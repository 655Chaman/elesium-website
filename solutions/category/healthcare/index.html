Deterministic Multi-Agent Architecture

Clinical healthcare environments demand infallible precision, near-instantaneous response times, and uncompromising patient data confidentiality. Generic cloud AI architectures that transmit patient health information (PHI) across public internet gateways violate both international HIPAA statutes and India's statutory Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023.

Elesium's clinical multi-agent systems operate within an air-gapped on-premise or sovereign private cloud enclave. Unstructured physician voice dictations, nursing handover notes, and pathology reports are ingested through a specialized Clinical De-Identification Agent that strips all 18 HIPAA Safe Harbor and ABDM identifiers before passing clinical semantics to the reasoning state graph.

Our diagnostic imaging agents interface directly with hospital PACS networks via standard DICOM C-STORE and C-MOVE protocols. Chest radiographs, brain CT scans, and ultrasound streams are processed by deterministic triage agents that detect intracranial hemorrhages, pneumothorax, and pulmonary nodules, immediately elevating critical cases to the top of the attending radiologist's worklist.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Elesium protect Patient Health Information (PHI) under India's DPDP Act 2023?
Patient identifiers are cryptographically tokenized and severed from clinical observations at the network ingress point. Furthermore, our models execute statelessly with zero persistence of audio or raw medical text.
Is the system compatible with India's Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM)?
Yes. Our architecture includes pre-certified ABDM milestone-1, 2, and 3 connectors for Ayushman Bharat Health Account (ABHA) creation, health document linking, and consent manager integration.
Can the agents integrate with our hospital's existing EHR/HIS software?
We support bidirectional communication with all major hospital information systems (Epic, Cerner, Meditech, KareXpert, Practo) via certified HL7 v2.x and FHIR R4 interfaces.
Are your diagnostic triage agents designed to replace certified radiologists?
No. Our agents act strictly as Tier-1 triage assistants that organize the radiologist's worklist by clinical urgency. All diagnostic reports require final clinician sign-off.
What hardware is required for on-premise hospital deployment?
A standard on-premise cluster consisting of 2x NVIDIA A100 or 4x L40S enterprise GPUs is sufficient to service a 500-bed hospital's concurrent imaging and clinical note throughput.
