## Executive Overview

This tracker provides a bird's-eye view of your entire lead pipeline—from the first sign of interest to a booked high-ticket sales call. It automatically aggregates data from your daily efforts across LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, X, and TikTok into a dynamic dashboard.

### Core Architecture

- **Omnichannel Clarity**
  Track prospects seamlessly across 7 different channels in one centralized location. Stop losing high-intent leads in your DMs.

- **Automated Analytics**
  The master Dashboard requires zero manual input. Log your daily interactions in the monthly tabs, and let the architecture calculate your conversion metrics dynamically.

- **Micro-Conversion Tracking**
  Pinpoint exactly where leads drop off with granular stage-to-stage conversion rates (A→B, B→C, C→D).

---

## The 4-Stage Pipeline

This tracker is built on a proven 4-step conversion architecture tailored specifically for high-ticket acquisition:

1. **(A) Interested**
   The prospect asked a question, made a request, or commented on your inbound content.
2. **(B) Engaged**
   You've initiated a deeper conversation and successfully sent your Native VSL or primary asset.
3. **(C) Calendly'd**
   You've successfully dropped your calendar booking link.
4. **(D) Booked**
   The prospect secured a time on your calendar.

## Implementation Guide

### 1. Secure Your Architecture
Make a private copy of the tracker to your own Google Drive workspace by clicking **File > Make a copy** on the original sheet.

### 2. Daily Operations
Navigate to the current month's tab (e.g., `Jan`, `Feb`). This operates as your daily system of record.
- When a prospect shows interest, log their name, date, and platform in the **Interested (A)** column.
- As they move through the funnel, move their details to the subsequent columns (**Engaged**, **Calendly'd**, **Booked**).

**Hygiene Protocols:**
- *Successfully Converted*: Strikethrough the text (do not delete the record).
- *Disqualified*: Strikethrough the text and highlight the cell.

### 3. Review Master Dashboard
Navigate to the **Dashboard** tab to view your macro-level analytics. 
- **Do not edit this tab.** It pulls all data dynamically using advanced matrix formulas.
- Review your conversion percentages to identify which platforms yield the highest quality leads.

## Advanced Metric Definitions

* **ABR %** (Overall Appointment Booking Rate)
* **A → B %** (Interest to Engagement): Measures how effectively you transition casual interest into a real, meaningful conversation.
* **B → C %** (Engagement to Link Sent): Measures how well your VSL or direct pitch performs at generating a calendar invite request.
* **C → D %** (Link Sent to Booked): Measures your link-click follow-through rate. If this is low, leads are clicking your link but experiencing friction.
