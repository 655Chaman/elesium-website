Frequently Asked Questions

How does the system verify Aadhaar data without violating UIDAI masking rules?
The platform utilizes UIDAI-compliant offline XML and secure QR code parsing, automatically redacting the first 8 digits of the Aadhaar number and storing only the masked identifier in encrypted storage.
Can the system detect deepfake video streams created with generative AI tools?
Yes. The liveness detection engine analyzes frame-by-frame temporal consistency, infrared depth variations, and biological micro-pulse signals that generative AI models cannot replicate.
Does this solution handle variations in Indian name spellings between Aadhaar and PAN?
Yes. The system incorporates customized Indian phonological matching algorithms (handling South Indian initials, patronymics, and honorifics) to eliminate false rejections.
