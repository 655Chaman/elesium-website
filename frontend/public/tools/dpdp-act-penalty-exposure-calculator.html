Mathematical Methodology & Benchmark Assumptions

1. Statutory Framework (Act No. 22 of 2023)

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 establishes significant financial liabilities under Section 33 and The Schedule for Data Fiduciaries and Significant Data Fiduciaries (SDFs):

  • Schedule Item 1 (Section 8(5)): Breach in observing reasonable security safeguards to prevent personal data breach — Statutory fine up to ₹250 Crores ($30.1M USD).
  • Schedule Item 2 (Section 8(6)): Failure to notify Data Protection Board of India and affected data principals of personal data breach — Statutory fine up to ₹200 Crores.
  • Schedule Item 3 (Section 9): Non-fulfillment of additional obligations in relation to children or individuals with disabilities — Statutory fine up to ₹200 Crores.
  • Schedule Item 4 (Section 10): Non-fulfillment of statutory obligations by Significant Data Fiduciaries (Periodic audits, DPIA, DPO appointment) — Statutory fine up to ₹150 Crores.
  • Schedule Item 5: Breach of any other provision or rule framed under the Act — Statutory fine up to ₹50 Crores.

2. Volumetric Liability Scaling Formula

Penalties are adjudicated by the Data Protection Board taking into account the nature, gravity, and duration of the breach, alongside the volume of data principals impacted:

Volumetric_Factor = min(1.0, log10(N_records) / 7.0)
Statutory_Exposure = min(250, max(Base_Risk, Sum(Violation_Weights)) * Volumetric_Factor)
