Mathematical Methodology & Benchmark Assumptions

1. The Edward Sturm Economic Sigmoid Framework

Traditional SEO agencies focus on high-volume top-of-funnel (TOFU) informational keywords with negligible commercial intent (conversion rates < 0.2%). The Programmatic BOFU model deploys thousands of highly specific long-tail operational queries with search intent directly correlated to procurement decisions:

Monthly_Visits = N_pages * 35 (Avg long-tail monthly visits)
Monthly_Demos = Monthly_Visits * 3.5% (BOFU demo conversion rate)
Annual_Pipeline = (Monthly_Demos * 12) * Annual_Contract_Value
Closed_ARR = Annual_Pipeline * Sales_Win_Rate
Payback_Days = (Pilot_Sprint_Investment / (Closed_ARR / 12)) * 30
