Deterministic Financial Formulas & Methodology

Enterprise technology investments require mathematical verification rather than speculative AI claims. Our return-on-investment framework is derived from audited production client data:

1. Reclaimed Labor Hours

Hours = FTEs × Weekly_Hours × 48 × Efficiency%

Standardized across 48 annual operational weeks, accounting for vacation and corporate holidays.

2. Net Annual Cost Savings

Net_Savings = Gross_Savings - VPC_Maintenance (12%)

Includes annual cloud GPU inference buffers, vector DB instances, and security patch updates.

3. Payback Breakeven Period

Payback_Months = Capex / (Gross_Savings / 12)

Calendar months required for accumulated recovered labor to equal initial system engineering fees.

4. 3-Year Capital Net Return

ROI% = [(3Y_Gross - 3Y_Cost) / 3Y_Cost] × 100

Compound cash-flow yield across 36 consecutive months of production autonomous state execution.
