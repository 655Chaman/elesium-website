4. Closed-Loop Shop-Floor ERP Synchronization: SAP S/4HANA PP/QM

A pervasive challenge in Indian manufacturing is 'phantom inventory' and delayed scrap reporting, where discrepancies between actual shop-floor scrap and ERP inventory records create sudden stockouts and assembly line starvation. Elesium's agents resolve this with reliable ERP integrations: 1. **Automated Scrap Booking:** When the machine vision inspection agent detects a non-conforming part, it triggers an event on the local Kafka bus that immediately invokes `BAPI_PRODORDCONF_CREATE_TT` in SAP S/4HANA, booking scrap against the exact work center in under 50ms. 2. **Dynamic Work Order Rescheduling:** If defect frequency on a CNC milling center exceeds 1.5% within a 15-minute rolling window, the orchestrator agent automatically recalculates tool wear matrices, reallocates high-priority parts to standby machinery, and flags maintenance teams via push alert before tool breakage occurs. 3. **Zero Paper Shift Handover:** Shift production summaries, overall equipment effectiveness (OEE), and maintenance logs are compiled autonomously from raw machine telemetry, eliminating subjective shift-end spreadsheets.
