3. OPC-UA Protocol Translation & Edge Telemetry Ingestion

The foundational backbone of modern industrial intelligence is the Open Platform Communications Unified Architecture (OPC-UA). OPC-UA provides a platform-independent, service-oriented architecture that standardizes industrial device data models, security encryption (X.509 certificates), and bidirectional telemetry exchange.

Elesium deploys localized OPC-UA aggregation servers at each factory facility. High-frequency analog signals (bearing acceleration, hydraulic pressure, spindle drive current) are sampled at 100Hz–1kHz at the edge. Dedicated sensor feature extractors calculate root-mean-square (RMS) velocity, peak-to-peak displacement, and Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) frequency spectrums directly in local memory, transmitting only compressed anomaly indicators and health index scores to upstream agents.
